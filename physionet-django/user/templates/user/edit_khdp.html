Korea Health Data Platform (KHDP) is a secure identity and data platform for global collaborative research. You can learn more at https://khdp.net/. If you do not already have a KHDP account, you can create one on the KHDP website.

If you already have a KHDP account, you can use the button below to link it to your PhysioNet account. When you link your account, KHDP may share the following information with PhysioNet so that we can keep your profiles in sync:

  • userId
  • userName
  • affiliation
  • mail
  • orcid
  • physionetId

Linking your KHDP account may help streamline identity verification and future integrations between PhysioNet and KHDP services.
