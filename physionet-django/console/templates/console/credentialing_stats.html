All application years, independent of the date filters above. Each application belongs to its submission-year cohort and is counted once under its current status. Handled = accepted + revoked + rejected; ever approved includes later revocations. The monthly charts instead group decisions by decision month.

Durations use fractional calendar days or hours, including zero, and exclude missing or negative intervals. Decision medians include handled applications with a valid decision date; pending and withdrawn cases are excluded. Recent cohorts are incomplete and may not be comparable with older cohorts. Each median shows its valid observation count (n).
