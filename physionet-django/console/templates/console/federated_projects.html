About Federated Projects

This page shows all projects cached from federated PhysioNet sites. These projects are automatically synchronized from remote instances and made searchable locally.

  • Cached Data: Only metadata is cached; files remain on the source site
  • Source URL: Click "View" to visit the project on its original site
  • Updates: Projects are refreshed during each sync operation

To manage federated sites or trigger a sync, visit the Federated Sites page.
