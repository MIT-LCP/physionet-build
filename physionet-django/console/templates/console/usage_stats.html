Registered file-access page viewers; not confirmed downloads or research use.

Definitions and limitations

Dataset access counts registered users viewing file-access pages. Access-log sessions overlapping the selected dates are included. Published versions are combined except in the per-version table.

It does not measure confirmed downloads or research use, and excludes anonymous access and direct cloud downloads.

Demographics use each person's latest approved credential application currently on record, not a historical snapshot. Countries are institutional countries, not inferred locations or nationalities. People without an approved credential application are listed separately from approved applications with missing or unrecognized information. Viewers, approved users and submitting authors are different populations.

Institution counts use self-reported names, ignoring case and extra spaces; abbreviations and spelling variations may still count as separate institutions. Access approvals cover contributor-reviewed requests, not DUA signatures or every access requirement. Research purposes are free text and are not classified here. Downloads by file, public landing-page visitors, regions and organization types are not measured here.
