Definitions and limitations

Contributor-reviewed dataset requests only. Requests are grouped by submission date; approvals by decision date, including later revocations and expiry. These are different cohorts and must not be interpreted as an approval rate or a count of users with current access. DUA signatures are not included.

Demographics use each person's latest approved credential application currently on record, not a historical snapshot. Countries are institutional countries, not inferred locations or nationalities. People without an approved credential application are listed separately from approved applications with missing or unrecognized information.

Institution counts use self-reported names, ignoring case and extra spaces; abbreviations and spelling variations may still count as separate institutions. Access approvals cover contributor-reviewed requests, not DUA signatures or every access requirement. Research purposes are free text and are not classified here.
